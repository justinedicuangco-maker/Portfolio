Simple Cookies

These simple cookies are soft, sweet, and easy to make with basic ingredients. Perfect for beginners and great as a snack or dessert.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter (softened)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix butter and sugar until smooth.
  3. Add egg and vanilla extract, then mix well.
  4. Combine flour, baking powder, and salt.
  5. Mix everything into a dough.
  6. Shape into small balls and place on tray.
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Let cool before serving.
